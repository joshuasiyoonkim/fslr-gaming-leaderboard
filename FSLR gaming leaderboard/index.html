Ranking Methodology

FOGSR™ Rating 3.2 — peer-reviewed by the peers being ranked

The Formula

FOGSR = [(CS2 × 0.45) + (TAC_FPS × APD × CC × 0.20) + (MOBA × 0.15) + (LEGACY × 0.20) + LTA + LAR] × MDC − OLE − DEC

Coefficient Definitions

  1. CS2 (45%) — Premier rating, normalized. Weighted heaviest, originally because it was the category in which the rankings were already correct. It has now twice produced results the committee did not anticipate and did not prefer. The weight remains 45%, because changing it at this point would look like exactly what it would be.
  2. LAR — Ladder Ascent Recognition (new, 3.2) — Where a player's Premier rating rises materially within a ranking period, the gain propagates through the CS2 weight without adjustment, cap, or committee discretion. Introduced in 3.2 not as a reward but as a disclosure: the committee wishes it on record that it did not decide Rae's promotion and could not have prevented it.
  3. DEC — Decorum Clause §13 (new, 3.2) — Statements made regarding a publisher, organizer, or opponent in the period immediately following elimination are assessed separately from play. The clause makes no provision for retraction, clarification, or apology, an omission the committee now regards as an oversight and declines to correct retroactively.
  4. §12.1 Sunset Provision (new, 3.2) — The Competitive Participation Bonus runs for one ranking period and then lapses. Entering a tournament is credited once; having entered a tournament is not a permanent condition. The committee considers this the clause's most important feature and the reason it works.
  5. Endurance Award (new, 3.2) — Sustained marathon play is credited strictly on outcome, at the rate the outcome implies. One hundred hours producing two rating points is credited at two rating points. The committee declines to pay by the hour and notes that no other industry does either.
  6. OLE — Off-Ladder Life Events (amended, 3.2) — A flat deduction for personal or professional developments outside competitive gaming. Marriage supersedes engagement and carries the new published maximum (−0.44). The committee reiterates that this measure is happiness-neutral and rating-negative, and extends its congratulations in the strongest terms the arithmetic allows.
  7. MDC — Micro Decay Coefficient — Mechanical skill is perishable; time away from ranked play is scaled ×0.82. Carried forward from 3.1, with the clarification that decay reverses on demonstrated return to the ladder. Two players triggered the reversal this period, by extremely different methods and to extremely different effect.
  8. APD / CC / LEGACY / LTA / Mecca Chameleon / Psyop §6.4 — Carried forward unchanged. The Psyop Clause saw no use this period, the committee noting that nobody attempted anything clever enough to invoke it.

Independence Statement

The FSLR Competitive Integrity Committee certifies that Rating 3.2 was not written to produce any particular outcome, and draws attention to the fact that it produced an outcome the committee spent two periods insisting was impossible. In August this body described the gap between #1 and #2 as the widest ever recorded and permitted the word unassailable into the minutes. That word has been struck. The committee further certifies that the Decorum Clause, the Sunset Provision, and the Endurance Award apply universally and to all players equally, and concedes without being asked that all three were drafted in the same week and that all three happened to land on the same man.

FOGSR™ is a registered trademark of nobody. Methodology subject to change retroactively, especially if Squeex loses at anything. Appeals may be submitted in writing and will be graded on penmanship, then discarded. Three of you have until December 31.
