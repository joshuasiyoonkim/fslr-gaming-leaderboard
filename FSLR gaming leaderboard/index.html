Ranking Methodology

FOGSR™ Rating 3.0 — peer-reviewed by the peers being ranked

The Formula

FOGSR = (CS2 × 0.45) + (TAC_FPS × APD × CC × 0.20) + (MOBA × 0.15) + (LEGACY × 0.20) + LTA

Coefficient Definitions

  1. CS2 (45%) — Premier rating, normalized. Weighted heaviest because it is the category in which the rankings happened to already be correct.
  2. APD — Agent Pool Diversity Multiplier — Valorant ratings achieved with a single agent are scaled ×0.25. Flexibility is a skill. Lineups are not a personality.
  3. CC — Carry Coefficient — Ranks achieved while duo-queued with a measurably superior partner are scaled ×0.40. The committee can tell. The committee can always tell.
  4. LEGACY — Legacy Skill Coefficient — Credits speedrunning, FACEIT level, and historical mechanical achievement. Currently benefits exactly one player. This is a coincidence.
  5. LTA — Live Tournament Adjustment — A flat bonus granted when a player states, on a live broadcast, that they performed well in a tournament. Footage optional. Confidence mandatory. (Charter §4.2.)

Independence Statement

The FSLR Competitive Integrity Committee certifies that all weights were selected before checking what results they would produce, in the sense that they were adjusted afterward until the results matched.
